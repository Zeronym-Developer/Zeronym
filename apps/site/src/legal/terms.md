## What this website is

This website explains Zeronym, a privacy protocol, and offers an app for its shielded pool on Robinhood Chain mainnet. The app runs in your browser and your keys stay with you: nothing on this website holds, moves or can recover your assets, and lost recovery material cannot be restored. Using the pool is at your own risk.

## What is official

A Zeronym contract or token is official only when its address is listed on the [Network and contracts](/docs/contracts/) page, and a feature is active only when its contract and records are published there. Anything else that claims to be Zeronym is not. The [Security](/security/) page explains how to check.

## No promises about privacy, yield or price

Zeronym does not promise absolute anonymity, yield on deposits or token price appreciation. Deposits and withdrawals are public on the host chain. The limits of each action are described in [What stays private](/docs/privacy/).
